# 🍕 Pizza Restaurant's Revenue Optimization Analysis

## Executive Summary

---

**Situation:**

The restaurant generated stable sales throughout the year, but revenue is concentrated in a small number of products, sizes, and peak operating periods.

**Complication:**

Large pizzas and a limited set of menu items drive a disproportionate share of revenue. Any stockout, slow service, or staffing shortage during peak hours directly impacts the restaurant’s highest-value sales.

**Question:**

How can the restaurant increase revenue and reduce operational risk without expanding the menu or increasing fixed costs?

**Recommendation:**

Focus inventory, staffing, and operational planning on large pizzas during lunch (12–1 PM) and dinner (5–7 PM) peaks, with particular emphasis on Fridays and the top-performing pizza portfolio.

**Expected impact:**

* Protect the restaurant’s primary revenue engine.
* Reduce stockout risk during peak periods.
* Improve service speed.
* Increase revenue from existing customer demand.

## 💡 Key Findings
- Large pizzas contribute 45.89% of revenue
- Lunch (12–1 PM) and dinner (5–7 PM) are peak revenue windows
- Fridays generate the highest revenue
- Top pizza portfolio creates concentration and stockout risk
